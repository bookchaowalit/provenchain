# ProvenChain — Discovery Plan

**Initiative:** PROVENCHAIN-DISCOVERY-001
**Owner:** Solo Empire founder
**Status:** draft / active
**Decision:** continue, narrow, or stop before testnet onboarding
**Evidence boundary:** anonymized interview summaries and aggregate counts only;
never store raw transcripts, customer documents, wallet secrets, or contact data
in this repository.

## Outcome and scope

Determine whether Thai SMEs and freelancers have a painful, recurring document
integrity problem that is valuable enough to test with a manual hash-and-verify
pilot.

### In scope

- Thai SME owners in B2B, export, contractor, and invoice-heavy workflows;
- freelance developers/designers and small agencies;
- document integrity, proof-of-existence, verification, trust, and willingness
  to try a manual pilot;
- privacy, legal, workflow, and buying constraints.

### Out of scope

- selling escrow or payment protection;
- collecting real customer documents;
- asking for wallet addresses, private keys, or identity documents;
- sending outreach or storing contact lists in this repository;
- treating interview statements as legal or market validation without evidence.

## Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence needed | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | A meaningful subset has experienced document dispute or proof-of-existence pain. | Recent example, consequence, current workaround, urgency. | At least 12 of 20 interviews confirm a concrete pain. |
| H2 | Independent verification is understandable and useful without blockchain expertise. | Participant can explain when a public verification link would change their workflow. | At least 10 of 20 describe a specific use case. |
| H3 | A manual pilot is worth trying before a full product exists. | Participant agrees to a bounded, non-sensitive sample flow. | At least 3 pilot candidates. |

## Interview plan

| Cohort | Target | Method | Duration | Status |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| SME owner | 10 | 1:1 conversation | 25–30 min | open |
| Freelancer/agency | 10 | 1:1 conversation | 25–30 min | open |

Use participant codes such as `SME-01` and `FREELANCE-01`. Keep names,
contact details, recordings, and raw notes in an approved private system, not in
this Git repository.

Use [INTERVIEW-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md](./INTERVIEW-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md) for
anonymous counts and aggregate findings only. For the legal/privacy handoff,
use [LEGAL-REVIEW-REQUEST.md](./LEGAL-REVIEW-REQUEST.md) and
[PIA-WORKSHEET.md](./PIA-WORKSHEET.md); neither document authorizes external
communication or a testnet transaction.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] 20 interviews completed across both cohorts;
- [ ] findings summarized by cohort without personal data;
- [ ] at least 12 concrete pain confirmations or an explicit failed hypothesis;
- [ ] at least 3 pilot candidates identified through approved private handling;
- [ ] no real customer document has been uploaded or submitted to a chain;
- [ ] recommendation recorded: proceed, narrow, pivot, or stop.
