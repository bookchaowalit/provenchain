## Handling rules

- Keep names, contact details, recordings, raw notes, and scheduling data in an
  approved private system, never in this repository.
- Use only participant codes such as `SME-01` and `FREELANCE-01` here.
- Store paraphrases only; never store customer document contents, screenshots,
  wallet addresses, identity documents, private keys, or secrets.
- A “pilot candidate” count does not authorize outreach, document collection,
  or a testnet transaction. The legal/privacy gate remains separate.
